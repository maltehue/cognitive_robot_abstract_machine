Weighed fill against the goal

Each dot is one pour; the diagonal is a perfect hit. Corn sits on it, styrofoam above it at every goal.

Where each pour's error comes from

Controller part: the fill the controller was told at the end minus the goal. Perception part: weighed minus told. They add up to the weighed error.

What arrives after the cup turns back

Each dot is one turn-back: how far the tilt fell before the stream stopped, and how much was counted after the peak, in shares of the source cup. Corn's count over-reads and styrofoam's under-reads, so corn's tails are drawn somewhat large and styrofoam's somewhat small.

Spill and peak tilt

Spilled mass as a share of the receiving cup. Corn's spill was 1 g on every pour, the scale's resolution. Styrofoam spills most when the cup passed 100°.

The bank's receiver belief at the end against the scale

What the hypothesis bank believed had arrived in the receiver when the pour ended, against the weighed fill. Above the diagonal the bank believed more than arrived.

The bank's source belief at the end against the scale

What the bank believed was left in the source cup, against one full cup minus what was weighed in and beside the receiver. Spill is invisible to the bank, so pours that spilled sit above the diagonal.

Largest fill the controller predicted against the outcome

The highest end-of-window fill the controller's model predicted during the pour. Corn's prediction runs far above what arrives, so the cup turns back early and creeps up again; styrofoam's lies closer, because the avalanches really do deliver it.

Did the bank correct the discharge coefficient?

The discharge coefficient cD (started at 0.2) sets only how far the stream is thrown, and the bank corrects it from where the camera sees the stream cross the opening. It does so only while the aim hypothesis (H2) leads. Filled dot: the factor on cD at the end of the pour; ring: its median while the count rose.

Fill told to the controller, fill counted at the opening, the wall reading (corn only), and the fill the controller predicted for the end of its 1.5 s window, against the source cup's tilt. The prediction is on the controller's clock, which starts with the transfer; it can lead the bank's clock by a fraction of a second.
